## 📌 Project Overview

The **Excel Sales Dashboard** is a data analytics and business intelligence project developed as part of my **Data Analytics Internship at SkillCraft Technology**.

The project focuses on analyzing sales data and transforming raw information into meaningful business insights through Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard provides a structured approach to understanding sales performance, identifying business trends, monitoring important metrics, and supporting data-driven decision-making.

By utilizing Excel's data analysis and visualization capabilities, this project demonstrates how raw sales data can be converted into meaningful and actionable business intelligence.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze sales data to identify meaningful business patterns.
- Monitor and evaluate overall sales performance.
- Identify trends and variations in sales.
- Transform raw data into meaningful visual representations.
- Develop a structured and informative sales dashboard.
- Support business decision-making through data visualization.
- Present analytical findings in an understandable format.
